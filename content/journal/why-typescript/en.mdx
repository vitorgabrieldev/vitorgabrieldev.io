Without types, you're trusting your team to remember to validate inputs at exactly the right moment. TypeScript enforces it. It's overhead in initial writing (15-20%), gains in confidence and refactor speed (3x faster). For teams: ts-node + tsx make the barrier zero. For maintainability: types are executable documentation that can't drift.
